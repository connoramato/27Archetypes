METHODOLOGY

How the test works

The creators of this test have no relevant credentials. All questions, answers, and scoring was made up willy-nilly.

Lover, Soldier, or Hero. This dimension represents your natural orientation toward other people and your role within a group.

For the Lover, depth and knowledge is the main end of all types of relationships,

For the Soldier, the main end of all types of relationships is togetherness, solidarity, and shared experiences. Values loyalty, comradery, and protecting the group.

For the Hero, the main end of all types of relationships is mutual growth and shared goals. Driven to act, overcome obstacles, and make a meaningful difference in the lives of others.

Pragmatist, Romantic, or Altruist. This dimension reflects the values and principles that tend to guide your decisions.

The main motivaiton and highest ideal in the Pragmatist's life is Truth. Practical, grounded, and interested in what actually works.

The main motivation and highest ideal in the Romantic's life is Beauty. Guided by their senses and the belief that all creation holds an unfolding mystery.

The main motivation and highest ideal in teh Altruist's life is Goodness. Led by compassion, generosity, and the desire to help.

Explorer, Wayfarer, or Dreamer. This dimension describes how you tend to interact with the world.

An Explorer is informed by the external and drawn toward new experiences and possibilities. They are focused outward and react accordingly.

A wayfarer is informed by an integrated sense of self and environment. They wonder as they wander. They are more comfortable with uncertainty, but stretch their focus between inward and outward.

A dreamer is informed by introspection, their imagination, and their inner wordls. They are focused inward and rely on mental processing.
